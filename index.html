About

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Cognitive Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Katharina von der Wense and Eliana Colunga, with a planned graduation in spring 2027.

My research lies in the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Computational Psycholinguistics, leveraging computational and statistical models to both understand and support language learning in young children. I also have interests in human-centered AI evaluation and multilingual NLP.

Outside of research, I enjoy tennis, hockey skating, and caring for my guinea pigs.
